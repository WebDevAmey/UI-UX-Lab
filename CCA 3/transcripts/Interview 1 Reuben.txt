UIUX Think Aloud Testing for 99acres

Interviewee 1
Name : Reuben Sam
PRN : 1032231018
Speech to Text transcription

Reuben Sam — Original 99acres Website:

“Okay, I'm opening the original 99acres website. I will try to find a property that matches my requirements and see how easily I can compare options and contact the owner.

First, I am looking at the homepage. I can see different property-search options, such as buying or renting a property. I will select the appropriate option and enter a location, such as Pune, to search for properties.

Now I am looking at the search results. I want to explore the available listings and check details such as the price, location, number of bedrooms, and property features. I can use the available filters to narrow down the results according to my requirements.

Next, I will open a property that interests me. I am checking its photos, price, location, and other available information to decide whether it meets my needs.

Now I want to see how easy it is to evaluate this property against other options. I will look for any available shortlist or comparison options and check whether I can compare the properties conveniently.

After that, I will look for the option to contact the owner or advertiser. I want to see how the website lets me make an enquiry or contact the person responsible for the listing. If a call or message option is available, I will try it and observe what happens."

Reuben Sam — Modified Figma Prototype:

“Now I am exploring the modified 99acres prototype. I will search through the available properties and see how easily I can find a property that matches my requirements.

First, I am looking at the property listings. I can browse the available options and check important details such as the price, location, and property features. I will open a property to get a better understanding of what it offers before deciding whether it suits my needs.

Next, I want to try the Compare Properties feature. This is useful because I may find multiple properties that look suitable, and comparing them can help me decide which one is better. I would like to compare their prices, locations, and other available details without having to remember everything from each listing.

Now I am going to check out a property by opening its details. I want to see whether the information is easy to find and whether I can understand the main features of the property without too much effort.

After that, I will try the Call Owner button. This option is useful when I want to speak directly with the owner and ask questions about the property, its availability, or arranging a visit. I will observe what happens when I click the button.

I will also try the Message Owner button. This gives me another way to enquire about the property if I prefer sending a message instead of making a call. I will check whether the process is clear and easy to follow. "

Reuben Sam – Final Conclusion

“So after operating on both websites i would say,

the prototype has a simpler, more focused interface, with convenient options to compare properties and contact owners through call or message buttons.

But at the same time prototype may offer fewer search filters and less flexibility when narrowing down properties based on specific requirements in comparison to the actual site.”
